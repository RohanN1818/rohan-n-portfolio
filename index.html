About

I'm an Electronics & Communication Engineer who builds on both sides of the wire — software that runs the logic, hardware that puts it in the physical world.

On the software side, I work across the Java Full Stack: designing REST APIs in Spring Boot, wiring them to React front ends, and getting the two sides to actually agree with each other — CORS, serialization, auth, the unglamorous plumbing that makes an app trustworthy.

On the hardware side, I design and build embedded systems — wiring sensors and RFID modules to microcontrollers like the ESP32 and engineering the logic that drives them. Software gives me room to think through a problem end to end; hardware forces that thinking to survive contact with the real world. I build with both, not one instead of the other.
